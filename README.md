# Hanzi Drill

Practice the 1,000 characters from *Your First 1,000 Chinese Characters*, or the HSK 3.0 vocabulary. Works offline once it has been opened online one time.

Pick the list under **Curriculum** on the start screen. The app reopens with whichever list you used last.

- **Top 1,000**: one character per day, in the book's order.
- **HSK 3.0**: the 11,068 entries of the HSK 3.0 exam syllabus published November 2025, which the HSK 3.0 exam uses from 13 December 2026. Levels 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7–9, most frequent words first within each level. Tap a level to practice it, or type word numbers. Multi-character words are drawn one character at a time in Write mode. A few words appear at more than one level because the syllabus lists different senses separately; the app shows them with the same meaning.

Each list remembers its own range and "learned up to" number. Which characters and words you miss is shared, so 人 counts the same in both.

- **Study**: browse cards in day order with each character's origin, parts, radical, traditional form and stroke-order animation. Swipe left or right.
- **Read**: see a character, pick its pinyin and meaning.
- **Recall**: see a meaning, pick the character and pinyin.
- **Write**: draw the character with your finger; each stroke is checked. Use "Parts hint" if you need it.

## Put it on GitHub Pages (about 5 minutes, easiest on a laptop or Chromebook)

1. Sign in at [github.com](https://github.com) (free account).
2. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**. Name it `hanzi-drill`, set it to **Public**, and click **Create repository**. (Free GitHub Pages needs a public repository.)
3. On the new repository's page, click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Unzip `hanzi-drill.zip`. Open the `hanzi-drill` folder and drag **everything inside it** into the upload area, including the `strokes`, `icons` and `licenses` folders. Click **Commit changes**.
   - Check that `index.html` sits at the top level of the repository, not inside another folder.
   - `.nojekyll` is a hidden file. If it doesn't upload, the app still works.
5. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**, pick **main** and **/ (root)**, and click **Save**.
6. Wait a minute or two, then open `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/hanzi-drill/`.

## Install it on your phone

1. Open the link above on your phone while you have internet.
2. Wait until **Available offline** appears under the title on the start screen. That means both lists and all their stroke data (about 10 MB) are saved on the phone.
3. Add it to your home screen:
   - **Android (Chrome)**: ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
   - **iPhone (Safari)**: Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Open it from the home screen icon. It now works in airplane mode.

## Updating later

If you change any file, open `sw.js` and bump the version (`hanzi-drill-v3` → `hanzi-drill-v4`), then upload the changed files.

`hsk.js` and `strokes/h0.json` … `strokes/h10.json` are generated by `node tools/build-hsk.js` (Node 18 or newer, needs internet). The sources are pinned to fixed versions, so it produces the same files each time. If a rebuild changes the number of `h*.json` files, update the count in `sw.js`. The `tools` folder isn't needed by the app. Phones fetch the new version the next time they open the app with internet, and use it from the launch after that.

## Notes

- Your progress (which characters you miss) is saved on each device separately. On iPhone, the home screen app and Safari also keep separate progress.
- "Hear it" uses your device's built-in Chinese voice. If you don't hear anything, install a Chinese (Mandarin) voice in your phone's text-to-speech settings.
