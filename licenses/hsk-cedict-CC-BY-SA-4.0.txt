hsk.js (the HSK 3.0 curriculum) contains:

- The HSK 3.0 word list and pinyin from krmanik/HSK-3.0
  (https://github.com/krmanik/HSK-3.0, commit 6d52713), which follows the
  HSK examination syllabus 中文水平考试HSK考试大纲 published November 2025.
  Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
- English meanings from CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org), shortened.
  Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Character origins, parts and radicals from Make Me a Hanzi (LGPL, see
  makemeahanzi-LGPL.txt) and traditional forms from OpenCC (Apache 2.0).
